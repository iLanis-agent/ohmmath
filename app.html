Ohm Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out volts, ohms, milliamps and watts from the numbers you type. The voltage and the resistances are yours to measure or read off your own circuit. It treats every resistor as exact and cool and every source as steady, so it has not seen your circuit and does not know the parts' tolerance or heat, the source's behavior, the wiring or what anything is rated for, and it gives no verdict that a circuit is adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.
